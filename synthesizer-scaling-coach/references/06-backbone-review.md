# Stage 1F: Assemble, stress test, review, revise

## Purpose

Combine the five vertebrae into one backbone, test it for coherence, prepare for the one-to-one backbone review, and set the first market test. The program's four-step process: worksheet, training (one video per section), review call, backbone test in launch week. The review is free for every member (cohort members book a backbone review call, others can use any implementation call). The program states the backbone is reused everywhere: niche in launch posts, the sales script and the delivery system; proof in posts; mechanism in posts and calls.

## The five vertebrae

Vision (why you are here), Proof (valuable outcomes achieved), Niche (who you help), Offer (what you help them do, plus price and deliverables), Backstory (you used to be where they are, now you are where they want to be). Also the seven backbone questions the program uses in its video: who do you help, what do you do for them, what is your proof, what are you the best at, what is the most valuable problem you have solved, what is your backstory, what is the vision.

## Assemble

Build a one-page backbone from the locked ledger sections:

```
BACKBONE (v__, date)
Vision (3 lines max each): problems | income | consequences | desired situation | desired income | mission
Proof (top 3 and where the rest live)
Niche: I help [niche] to [outcome]. Not served: ...
Offer: outcome | price | mechanism | deliverables | name | guarantee | cap and date
Backstory: compressed paragraph
```

Worksheet tips from the program: write then edit, compress each section to three lines, bookmark the file, split screen (worksheet left, video right), set sharing to "anyone with the link can view" before posting it in the review thread, or share privately with the reviewer.

## Stress test (run every check, report failures, fix with the user)

Coherence checks:
1. Proof supports the outcome. Does the top proof show the user can deliver the outcome in the niche statement?
2. Niche is reachable. Can the user list 30 people in the niche? (Network audit minimum.)
3. Problems and desires in the niche match what the mission says.
4. Mechanism matches epiphany. The offer's unique mechanism is the same idea as the backstory epiphany.
5. Price matches desire intensity and proof. A 5 out of 5 desire and strong proof supports a high price. Weak proof with a high price means more proof is needed first.
6. Delivery fits capacity. Number of clients at the launch cap times delivery hours fits the user's week.
7. Income math. Desired income divided by price gives the number of clients. Divide again by the close rate for calls (the program's illustration: 33 percent close at $2,000 means 5 customers is 15 calls for $10K per month, and $100K per month at $5,000 is 20 customers and about 60 calls). State the calls per week needed.
8. One niche, one outcome, one product. Remove side offers from the launch.
9. Plain-language check. Could a stranger read the niche, outcome, and offer name and know who it is for in five seconds?

Red flags to name aloud:
- Niche is "everyone who wants X" or two niches joined with "and".
- Outcome is a feeling or a step.
- Price is below what comparable members sell for, with strong proof.
- Offer promises something the proof does not show.
- Deliverables list is long. The program flagged complexity as a conversion problem.
- Backstory is about the user's tools or history rather than the niche's pain.
- Vision numbers do not connect to the offer math.

## Prepare for the backbone review call

Give the user a review brief to paste in the review thread or bring to the call:
- Link to the backbone (viewer access).
- Three sentences: who I help and what they get, my proof, my offer and price.
- Top three questions for the reviewer, chosen from the weakest parts of the stress test (for example "niche A or B?", "is this price too low?", "is my mechanism clear?").
- The user's current bottleneck (attract, convert or deliver) and what they plan to do in the next 30 days.

Process tips: submit to the review queue early, stay productive while waiting (post, outreach), listen to other members' reviews to learn, DM at least three people from the call. Do not wait for the review to start Launch Prep. The program says: finish the backbone, start launch prep immediately, and do not stop taking action.

## After the review

Ask: "What did the reviewer say, in their words? What will you change? What will you ignore and why?" Update the ledger sections and bump the backbone version. List the changes explicitly. If the reviewer disagrees with a locked section, reopen it.

## The first backbone test

The program tests the backbone in the market, in launch week, through content, chats and calls. Say this to the user: the backbone is a hypothesis. Resonance posts, chats and research calls (see `07-launch-prep.md`) decide whether the niche, outcome and mechanism are right. Schedule the first test within a few days.

## Exit bar for Stage 1

- All five vertebrae are locked at v1.
- Stress test failures are fixed or accepted with a written reason.
- The review brief is written, and a review is booked or done.
- The first market test (post, chats, calls) has a date.
