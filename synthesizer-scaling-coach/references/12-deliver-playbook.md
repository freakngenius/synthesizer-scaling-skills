# Stage 2F and 5: Deliver playbook

Read when the user has a sale or is about to, and asks how to run the program, onboard, or build content. Full notes in the library skill: `notes/2-launch-deliver-1-program-outline.md`, `2-launch-deliver-2-onboarding.md`, `2-launch-deliver-3-build-as-you-go.md`, `2-launch-deliver-4-action-steps.md`.

Exit bar: a 3 to 6 phase program outline, a recorded welcome video, and the next delivery week built (and only that week).

## Principles

- A program is a guided sequence that produces a transformation, not a pile of information. Delete anything that does not support the promised result.
- Build as you go (just in time). Outline the phases, record the onboarding welcome video, then build only the next delivery week before it begins. For each phase decide what the client must do first, then derive the modules and training that support those actions. Watch questions and progress, and let that shape next week. A Google Doc plus one-to-one guidance is enough for a first version. Do not delay delivery to build a polished course, community or platform.
- Program outline: use the Backbone and the pitch pillars, name 3 to 6 milestones simply, stop. Modules and action steps come later.

## Onboarding (after payment, before start)

Goals, from the lesson: affirm the decision (validation), orient to the mission (vision), set the identity and responsibilities that produce results (identity), restate the outcome, explain the roadmap (clarity), show proof (confidence), clarify expectations, and give an immediate first action (momentum). Eight named outcomes: validation, vision, identity, outcome, clarity, confidence, expectations, momentum. Format: one reusable welcome video for group or one-to-many programs; a customized onboarding call plus document for one-to-one. Keep it energetic and simple. Templates are in the Onboarding Training doc (library master index, "2. Onboarding").

## Coaching moves

- Ask which delivery model they have sold (one-to-one, group cohort, hybrid) and keep them to it until clients are in.
- If they want to build a course, community or platform before a client exists, refuse politely and send them back to booking calls.
- If clients are stuck or waiting, the Attraction calls cover cohort activation, evergreen onboarding, and early milestones. Pointers: library notes for "Dissecting Her $1M Skool Business", "Copy and Paste This AI Prompt To Unlock Scaling Secrets", "4 Brand NEW Skool Scaling Tactics". Treat the experiments in those calls as early tests the hosts did not call validated.
- Delivery overload (about 5 to 10 clients and delivery eating over 50 percent of time) is a Scale-stage constraint. See `13-diagnosing-and-post-launch.md`.
