# Post Launch Protocol
Type: lesson   Stage: Scale   Length/size: Written protocol; no duration stated
## One-paragraph summary
This document gives a weekly operating system for continuing after the initial launch. It organizes work into attract, convert, and deliver, then lists a recurring activity cadence for each area. It directs members to use the supplied scaling system, calculator, routine tracker, and lesson resources rather than inventing new marketing or sales approaches. Members are told to innovate on the product while following the given attract and convert process, and to maintain focus for 12 to 24 months. It also distinguishes when a coaching offer can be sold immediately from a cohort offer that should wait until its core program exists, and gives a follow-up script plus a steep discount-and-testimonial suggestion for launches with no sales.
## Core ideas and frameworks (named concepts, each with its steps or components, as taught)
- **The scaling system:** The document points to a simplified system, a full scaling system, and a customization resource. It does not spell out the system's detailed mechanics in this file, but places it alongside the target calculator and weekly routine as the operating framework for post-launch work.
- **Attract, Convert, Deliver weekly routine:** Attract creates attention through one YouTube video per week, Skool networking, three posts per week, and 30 comments per day. Convert turns engagement into conversations and sales through a free Skool group, three posts per week, 30 comments per day, one call per week, daily outreach, group joins and daily sales calls. Deliver improves the program and supports current customers through weekly program improvement, daily customer success, two group calls per week, 1:1 calls, and weekly check-ins.
- **One Source Rule:** The stated rule is that the routine contains what is needed to reach 6 to 7 figures and that anything outside the list should not be done. Members are told not to innovate on attract or convert, but to innovate on the product and deliver strong results. This is presented as a focus and adherence rule.
- **Post-launch selling decision:** A coaching offer can be sold straight away. A cohort offer should wait until its core program is built. The file does not define the distinction between coaching and cohort beyond this timing instruction.
- **No-sale recovery sequence:** Reach back out to everyone spoken with during launch, reconnect with a personal check-in, refer to the conversation and desired outcome, introduce an offer, and ask whether the person wants more information. If interested, invite them to book a call. The document separately suggests dropping the price by 90% in exchange for a testimonial, with the stated explanation “We just need to get you momentum.”
## Tactics and how-tos (concrete, ordered, include any numbers, thresholds, timings, tools, settings)
1. Use the linked Math to $100,000/month calculator and the Simplified System or Full Scaling System. Use “Customize your System here” if the full system needs customization. The actual URLs are not included in this text.
2. Set an operating week using the categories and quantities below. The same post and comment counts appear in both Attract and Convert, so retain them as written rather than assuming one category replaces the other.
3. For Attract, make one YouTube video each week. The listed production workflow is to generate 10 ideas, select an idea, choose title, thumbnail, and hook, brain-dump, write an outline, record, edit, and upload. Also do Skool networking, publish three posts per week, and leave 30 comments per day.
4. For Convert, run a free Skool group, post three times per week, leave 30 comments per day, make one call per week, send 100 outreach messages per day, join groups, and hold sales calls daily. The document identifies possible outreach sources as group members and people who engage with posts or comments. It also says to reach people in Skool communities and use Instagram when a Skool limit is reached.
5. For Deliver, improve the program weekly. Work on the program outline, training, and over-delivers. Handle customer success daily, respond to DMs and comments, run group calls twice weekly, hold 1:1 calls, and conduct weekly check-ins.
6. Keep following the listed routine for 12 to 24 months. The document ties this period to staying focused, but does not specify a review cadence or how to adjust targets.
7. If no sales came from launch, contact each person spoken with using the follow-up wording below. When a person expresses interest, send the booking instruction and call link. The actual scheduling URL is absent.
8. Choose the next sale timing based on delivery format: sell a coaching offer immediately; for a cohort, finish building the core program before selling.
