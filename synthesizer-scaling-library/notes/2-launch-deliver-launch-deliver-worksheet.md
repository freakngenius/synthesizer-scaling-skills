# Launch Deliver Worksheet
Type: worksheet   Stage: Launch Deliver   Length/size: Worksheet; no duration stated
## One-paragraph summary
The worksheet is a launch workspace organized around the user's business backbone, a launch calendar, links to training and templates, and checklists for Launch Prep, Launch Attract, Launch Convert, and Launch Deliver. It prompts the user to define vision, proof, niche, premium offer, and backstory, then track dates and action completion. Its purpose is to keep positioning and offer inputs alongside concrete launch actions, rather than treating delivery as a separate process detached from the launch. The opening instructions say to make a copy of the source file and set sharing so anyone with the link can view. The checklist includes numeric commitments such as contacting 30 or more network members and optionally conducting 5 or more research calls.
## Core ideas and frameworks (named concepts, each with its steps or components, as taught)
- **Business Backbone:** A set of five sections that clarify the business before and during launch.
  - **Vision:** Why the user is here; current problems and income; consequences; desired situation and income; mission.
  - **Proof:** Three proof entries showing what the user has achieved for themselves or others. The worksheet suggests adding images.
  - **Niche:** Who the user serves, that audience's current situation, problems and desires, and who the user does not serve.
  - **Premium Offer:** Outcome, price, unique mechanism, deliverables, name, guarantee, and scarcity or urgency.
  - **Backstory:** Past situation, past pains, past desires, epiphany, and current situation.
- **Launch calendar:** A date-planning area for Prep, Attract, Convert, and Deliver, each with a start and end date. The form does not prescribe durations.
- **Phase-based launch checklist:** Each phase has named training or resources and a sequence of action items. Launch Prep covers audits, prep posts, network outreach, and optional research calls. Launch Attract covers calendar setup, launch posts, and follow-up with engagers and the network. Launch Convert covers sales preparation and pitching. Launch Deliver covers program outline, welcome video, and building as the program proceeds.
- **Resource map:** Paired labels identify relevant resources for audits, messaging, scripts, calendar setup, sales, onboarding, and program building. Several labels repeat the training title as the link name, and the actual URL destinations are not included in this text.
## Tactics and how-tos (concrete, ordered, include any numbers, thresholds, timings, tools, settings)
1. Make a copy of the worksheet using File → Make a Copy. Then use Share → Anyone with the link can view. These are the worksheet's specific document setup instructions.
2. Fill in the five Backbone sections. Record concrete proof and include images when available, then write the niche's situation, problems, and desires, and identify who is outside the intended audience. For the offer, capture the outcome, price, mechanism, deliverables, name, guarantee, and scarcity or urgency. The worksheet provides fields but no word counts or evaluation criteria.
3. Enter dates for the start and end of each launch phase: Prep, Attract, Convert, Deliver. The worksheet does not give a recommended number of days for any phase.
4. For Launch Prep, watch all training, complete the Network Audit and Audience Audit, create Launch Prep Posts 1, 2, and 3 using the Audience Audit, and contact 30 or more people listed in the Network Audit. Conduct 5 or more research calls if choosing the optional research-call activity.
5. Use the linked Opportunity Audit, Network Audit + Audience Audit, Message Resonance, Backbone Worksheet, Launch Prep Content Templates, Launch Prep Chat Script, and Launch Prep Research Call Script as applicable. The worksheet names these resources but does not include working URLs in the source text.
6. For Launch Attract, watch all Launch Attract training, set up Calendly to book calls, make the launch posts and mark them in the Audience Audit, and reach out to people who engage with those posts as well as every person in the Network Audit.
7. For Launch Convert, watch all Launch Convert training, duplicate the Sales Script Template, use training to fine-tune and practice the pitch, and pitch on a call. The resources include sales script, discovery, pitch, objection handling, and a sales call tracker.
8. For Launch Deliver, watch all Launch Deliver training, create a program outline, film a welcome video, and build one week at a time as you go. The later lesson clarifies that the initial outline need only show the phases, not every detail, but this worksheet itself simply lists the outline task.
9. Use the check column to mark action completion. The worksheet does not prescribe a review cadence or define who else should have access beyond the view-link instruction.
