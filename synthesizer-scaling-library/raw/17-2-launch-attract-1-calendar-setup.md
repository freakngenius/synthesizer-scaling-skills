* Qualify leads
* Improve show-up rates


How to set up Calendly (Free)


Step 1: Create a new event
* Create an account at calendly.com 
* Click “Create” and “New 1:1 event type”
* Set duration to 45 min
* Add either google meet or zoom location
* Set your availability 
   * Invitees can schedule “7 days” into the future with at least “2hrs notice”
   * OR- Within a date range: “14 July - 21 July, 2025”
* Schedule booking hours 
   * Recommended: Mon - Saturday
   * Set times you are available - The more times you set the higher your booking and show rate. Be aware of timezones.
   * People book in more on weekends and after/before work hours.
   * At peak I did 7am - 8pm availability with a break at 1:00pm and 6:00pm


Step 2: Customize and qualify
* Click “more options” 
* Edit description to explain the purpose of the call: 
   * This is a 45 min Strategy call to determine if we're a good fit to work together. If I can help you, I'll walk you through how. If not, I'll make sure you leave with clarity on your next steps.
   * Please answer in detail. We may cancel calls we do not think are a good fit.
* Add a 15min buffer after each event
* Set booking page increments to show in increments of 60 min
* Automatically detect and show the times in my invitees time zone
* Set up qualifying questions on the Invitee Form. Note: This will be different for everyone. Think about what information you need from them in order to make a decision if they are going to be a good fit.
   * Name
