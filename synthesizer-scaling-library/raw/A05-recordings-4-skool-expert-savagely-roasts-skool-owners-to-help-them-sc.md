Purpose: 
* Provide clear training on what a continuity/upsell offer is and how to sell it.


Definitions:
* Synthesizer Continuity offer: More/longer of the same thing they have already purchased at the lesser price/time ratio.
   * Ex: Annual upgrade.
* Synthesizer Upsell offer: NEW + more longer at significantly higher price to time ratio. Generally comes with smaller circles, more access, better training. 
   * Ex: Mastermind, 1:1 access, in person events, guest speakers etc.
   * We will ignore this for now. You should only worry about this once you have 50+ clients and multiple success stories. Otherwise it will be a distraction.


Continuity + Upsell Pricing Commandments:
* 2x Price 2x Time = industry standard for Upsells
   * Ex: main offer = $3,000 for 6 months. Continuity = $6,000 for 12 months.
   * BUT potential for massive LTV lifts: 
      * Ex: main offer = $3,000 for 6 months. Upsell Mastermind = $15,000
* 1.5x price 2x Time = no brainer 
   * Ex: main offer = $3,000 for 6 months. Continuity = $4,500 for 12 months.
* Significant price reduction + long term commitment = easy to sell continuity 
   * Ex: Main offer = $3,000 for 6 months. Continuity = $250/m $2,000/Year


Continuity + Upsell Selling Commandments:
*  Add bonuses they would pay for outside of continuity
   * More access (1:1 calls) 
   * More training (bonus/future training)
   * New + exciting (events, merch, meetups, status)
* Use price Anchors: “Price will be raising to $4,000 for 6 months. This means it would be $8,000 for a year. But you can lock in lifetime pricing if you upgrade at $6,000/12 months”
* Use scarcity/urgency: This is only available for the next 24hrs. I do this so we can refocus on getting you results and move on.
* When to sell: Only sell continuity after they have achieved a core deliverable/result/milestone of initial offer or 2-3 weeks out of program ending


Core Continuity Retention Pillars:
* If your first thing sucked, they won’t buy again
* Overdelivery on your promises creates loyalty
* Community is key: The connections people form are irreplaceable and are painful to lose
* Unique Mechanism is key: If clients follow you as the leader, and trust your way of doing things, they will want to continue following that way.
* Be a Synthesizer: Stay ahead of your clients. Solve the problems in front of them and you will be able to continue to help them.

1. Core milestone reached: If they reach a milestone (eg first client/$10k/month) 
2. Time Elapsed: If you are 2-3 weeks out of program ending
