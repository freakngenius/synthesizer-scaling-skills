## Doc: Sales Script

Synthesizer Scaling Sales Script
Pre Call Prep
* Background tidy, Hair and clothes clean
* Quiet room free from interruptions, headphones in 
* Script printed in front of you with pen handy
* Review prospect’s application, social profiles, and relevant information
* Remember to record the call 
* 6 physiological sighs: Deep breathe in, hold, sharp additional inhale through nose, slow exhale through mouth
* Release all attachment and need to “close”, you are here to help and accurately diagnose
* Join the call right on the minute on time. This should be a 45 min call. 30-60 min range.
Intro & Framing
“Hey [name], great to connect! Where are you calling from?” (light small talk, smile be happy)


“Cool. Well I know we don’t have too much time, so let’s jump in. Do you have something to take notes on if needed?” 


“Awesome. So probably what would be most helpful, is I’ll ask you a couple of questions around where you’re at, what you’re working on, and where you want to go. Then if it sounds like we’re a good fit, I can walk you through what it looks like to work together and at the end you can decide if you want to be part of it or not! Sound good?”


Discovery
A. Primary Motivation
“Perfect, so maybe, the best place to start is, looking at what got you interested in booking this call in the first place?”


IF booked through DMs: 
“Perfect, so I know we’ve chatted in the DMs already a little, but it would probably be helpful to ask… what got you interested in booking this call in the first place?”
If you get nowhere with the first question:


“When you say ___, what do you mean exactly?”
“Could you explain that in a little more depth?”
“Sweet, well props to you for taking action and getting to where you are now… now let me ask you this…”


B. Current Situation Diagnosis
"What are you doing for work right now?"
"What do you sell? How do you price it?"
"How are you currently [doing things they want eg: getting customers right now]?"
"Do you have a system in place for [insert thing they want]"
"Are you comfortable with where things are at with [insert what they are working on]?"
C. Pain & Problem Diagnosis
"What do you think is the biggest challenge right now?"
"Why do you think you haven’t solved it yet?"
"What have you tried already?"
"How long has this been going on?"
D. Desired Situation Diagnosis
“Ok, [name], well what is your 12 month goal for the [thing]?”
“Whats your motivation for getting to [goal]?
“How would getting to [goal] impact other areas of your life?”
        “What else would change in your life if you reached [goal]?”
        “Lets say, next month you hit [goal], what would be different?” 
“Okay [name]. Well you currently are at [Current situation] and you want to be [goal situation]. Now, correct me if I’m wrong… but it seems to me that if you had a proven roadmap to follow and a mentor to walk you through that who has done it before… you would probably be the type of person to take action on that?”
E. Cost of Inaction
“Okay, well how has struggling with [problem] been having an impact on you?”
"What happens if nothing changes over the next 6 months?"
“Ok if we were to flash forward 6 months from now, and things were still how they are currently, is that possibly something you want to avoid?”
"What is that costing you in time, energy, or money?"
"Now if we were to go back in time 6 months ago and you had a clear roadmap and possibly mentorship to help you, do you think you’d maybe not be struggling with [current situation] and be closer to [desired situation] today?"
“Okay cool. Now it sounds to me like you are pretty committed… but just to double tap this… how committed are you to making this happen?”
Transition


“Great. I feel like I have a solid understanding of where you’re at, but before we potentially move on, what else should I know about you or your situation?”


“Awesome. Well [name], based on everything you’ve told me I 100% believe we can help you because [INSERT REASONS based on what they’ve told you AND YOUR PROOF]”


“Would you like me to walk you through what we do and how it works?”
Pitch
"Awesome. Now, this is a [3]-step system to take you go from [current situation] to [desired result] in about [timeframe]."
“And would you mind writing down [1-3] in your notes? Just so we make sure we don’t miss anything.”
Pillar 1: [Custom Name]


“The first pillar is called [name]. You see, after [proof] we’ve noticed that most people are doing [old way]... which leads to [problems], which results in [painful thing].  What we do instead is [pillar description], so you can [benefit] and [benefit]”. What stands out to you about that?
Pillar 2: [Custom Name]
Now that we have taken care of [pillar 1] you’ve already got [benefit] and benefit] and are ready for our second phase, [pillar 2 name]. Here we’ve noticed after [proof] that most people need [xyz]. So that's why we do [solution] so you can get [result]. What questions do you have about that? 
Pillar 3: [Custom Name]


“Now at this point in time you’re finally ready for [pillar 3]. Here, where we noticed people go wrong is once they have [pillar 1] and [pillar 2] setup they stop… This means they reach a ceiling on their growth they can’t get past. So what we do instead is [final mechanism]. This is actually what allowed one of my clients to [stop doing painful thing] and start [doing things they want] which ultimately resulted in [benefit of benefit]. Does that make sense?”
Delivery


“Now how this all works is first we will get you set up with [first deliverable.] This lets you get setup and get results right out of the gate. Now you also get [second deliverable] which means you will never get lost and will always have the 1:1 support you need. On top of all that you also get [third deliverable]. This means after everything starts working you have exactly what you need to continue taking this to the next level.”


Commitment 
“What stands out to you most about this so far?” 

“Which part of this do you feel most compatible with?” 


“Okay cool. Now, on a scale of 1-10… with a 10 being wow, you are so amazing and wonderful get me in there right now and a 1 being you suck I can’t wait to get off this call [laugh], where would you sit in terms of confidence on that right now?” 
        
        “Nice. Well, why not a 1?” 
        “Okay cool, now what would it take to make this a 10 for you?”


*When given a concern: Acknowledge the concern, associate with buying behaviour, ask a question
        
“Great question. I’ve noticed a lot of my successful students come in asking the exact same thing so I appreciate you saying that. Now when you say ___ what exactly do you mean by that?”


“Okay cool. So what we do differently is we actually [do thing they want] which gives [benefit]. Does that answer your question?”
Investment 
“Sweet. Now what other questions do you have for me? Maybe about next steps or how to get started?”


“Awesome. So typically what happens is we will just take care of the investment live on the call, then that gives you immediate access to [thing they want], and I can walk you through the next couple steps live right now on the call.”


“I know this is worth/I will be charging [1.5x AMOUNT]. But since our main priority is [EXCUSE eg: since our main priority is to get the right people in and get incredible case studies] for the next [5/#] people, the investment is only [$X].”
*shut up, stop talking, confident eye contact, no justification
Objections & Close


If yes:


“Sweet. So what I will do now is send over a custom payment link in the chat of this call. Then we can get you immediately inside so we can take action on [next step] right away.”


If anything but a yes: WE DO NOT ACCEPT ANYTHING BUT A YES OR NO.


“Cool! What is potentially holding you back from joining something like this right now?”


“Awesome. What else?” (Keep asking this until you have isolated an objection. Then handle below) 




Budget: I can’t afford it


“Totally man. I completely respect that. Now, just for clarity… finances out of the question, what else would hold you back from joining something like this?”
        
                BOOM. They say the new objection, move to handling that one. 


“So if you had the money in your hand right now, you would 100% be ready to rock and roll?”


“Okay… Why would you do it?”


“Well let me ask you this… earlier you mentioned this is exactly what you need. Do you think if we went back 6 months ago and you had this you would be in a better position today?”


“Okay man, well what's more risky? Repeating the mistakes of the past, or for you to overcome your limiting belief about money so you can invest and never worry about it again?”


“Okay man, well how can we make this work for you?”


** Downsell payment plan
        
“Now, let me ask you this, is it the full amount that's the hang up? Would it help if we looked at a payment plan?”


“Cool. Well we don’t offer this to everyone… but because I’m confident we can get you results… what I can do for you is [3 month split, 2 payment split, etc]”




Authority: I need to speak to spouse/business partner


** Prevent this early on. Either get them in on the call right away, or rebook to when they are there. Half the time it's a smoke screen. If it's real, BAMFAM.


“100%. You should definitely talk to your spouse about this. Now, are they generally supportive of you investing in achieving your goals?”


“Okay cool. Well what questions would they potentially have for me? Would it be about the process, the price, or something else?” 


                BOOM. They say the new objection, move to handling that one. 


** If they really need to talk to their spouse/partner…


“Cool man, well where is your spouse right now? Are they around?”


“Well great! Call them in here, let's make sure we are all on the same page.”


“No? Well when do you think you would both be free to go over this?” (Book a meeting for that time and date

“Awesome, I definitely want you to think about this. I’ve noticed all of our best clients are ones who don’t rush into decisions. Now, on this topic, what part of all of this feels least compatible for you right now?”


“Cool man, I respect that. Now, generally I’ve found that it doesn’t take time to make decisions… It takes information. And since I’m the only source of information on this program, what's holding you back from jumping right in today? Is it around the process, the price, or maybe something else?”


“Perfect, let's think about it. Now just because I want to be respectful of your time, what questions do you think might pop up in your mind you want answered after this call?


        BOOM. They say the new objection, move to handling that one.

“Cool man, now what makes you think that this is something that could resolve that for you?”


“Now, let me ask you this… do you think waiting and hesitating to make decisions that you clearly think will help you achieve your goals could be one of the reasons you’re stuck where you are?”


        “Well, why make the mistakes of the past? Let's rock and roll.” 


** If they still need to think about it… jump to guarantee.
