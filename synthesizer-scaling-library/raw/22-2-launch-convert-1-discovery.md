2 . Discovery: The Gap Framework


* People do not buy coaching, accountability, programs, or even you. 
* They buy because there is a gap between where they are and where they want to be
* Sales is simple. Help the lead identify the gap between where they are and where they want to be and then sell them the bridge.
* You need to agitate the pain. Make them feel it. Your goal is to find the wound and pour salt into it to make them cry. 
* Then make the desire vivid and tangible. Help them imagine their dream life and how much better their life could be.
  



The 5 Discovery Checkpoints of The Gap Framework: 
1. Primary Motivation
2. Current Situation 
3. Pain and Problems 
4. Desired Situation
5. Cost of Inaction 




Why do people buy? 
* To bridge the gap faster
* Because they don’t know how to bridge the gap on their own 
* Because they want a roadmap to follow and a guide to walk them down it
