## Doc: Onboarding Training with templates

Onboarding Training




Purpose of this training: 
* Create your onboarding for new clients. 


What is onboarding?
* The time period after payment before the program itself starts
   * Good onboarding results in clients who are bought in and ready to take things seriously and take action.
   * Bad onboarding results in clients who churn, don’t show up, and fail to take action
* We use onboarding time to work on the program to come and to complete the following checklist:




Successful Onboarding Checklist:
* Validation - Celebrate exclusivity and affirm purchase decisions.
* Vision - Frame the big problem and share the mission of why they are here.
* Identity - Define the ideal identity you want people to adopt to get the best results. 
* Outcome - Share your story with past struggles and your current situation.
* Clarity - Present the outline with the phases you created in the previous lesson.
* Confidence - List wins with names, numbers and results for social proof and credibility.
* Expectations - What your clients can expect from you, and what you expect from them
* Momentum - What is the next step they can take?


Deliverables:
* If you are doing 1:1
   * Program
   * Weekly call
   * Personal number/24hr support
* If you are doing 1:many
   * Weekly group call 
   * Weekly pre recorded training drop on modules
   * Community asynchronous support
   * 1:1 support (if needed)
