Influential: They significantly influence a person's thoughts, emotions, and actions, dictating their responses to various situations. Emotionally Charged: They often have a strong emotional component and can be tied to a person's sense of self-worth and existential outlook. 


2. Secondary Beliefs: Secondary beliefs are more specific and situational beliefs that derive from or interact with core beliefs.
