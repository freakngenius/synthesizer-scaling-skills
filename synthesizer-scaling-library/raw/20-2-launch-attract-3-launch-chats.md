Conversation Spark

Problems

Step 1: Conversation Spark
Right off the bat, you want to make it easy for them to reply. So start with something non-threatening and unique. (I like to call people sir or lady)


Hello good sir…
   * “Thanks for the love on my post! Curious, what resonated with you the most there?”
   * “Thanks for the comment! What takeaways did you have?”
   * “Thanks for the like! What stood out to you the most on that post?”
   * “Hey [Name]! Appreciate the like.” 




Step 2: This/That
Now you’re going to start qualifying but it’s still early enough that you want to stay non-threatening.


   * “Are you looking at [primary objective] or [secondary objective]?”
   * “Do you already have a [solution related to your offer] or are you looking at setting one up?




Step 3: Desired Situation
Now you’re going to qualify further.


   * What's the goal? 
   * Where do you want to be 6 months from now with that?
   * What is your main priority right now [topic] wise?




Step 4: Problems


This question is very simple, no need to overcomplicate it. 


   * “Gotcha. What do you think you need the most right now to get [goal]?
   * “Understood. What seems to be the biggest challenge in getting that done for you right now?”


This is a great question because if you can provide what they need (if it’s literally your service), then you know they’re a good fit.


Step 5: Help


You know what they need so the next step is simple:


   * “Would you like some help?”
   * “Would you be opposed to [seeing how we can get you your dream outcome] this week?”




Step 6: Sell the Call


Pick a date and time or send them your calendly:
   * Awesome. Are you free right now? I got some space to hop on… 
   * “Awesome. Are you free [day/time] or would it be easier if I send my calendar over?”
   * Amazing. Grab a spot on my calendar here. [LINK] I just opened up a few extra spots for you.


Give them a reason to book right there and then.


   * “Cool, I’ll keep my window open while you choose a time. Mind telling me when you’ve found one that works?”
   * “Cool, let me know what email you book with so I can make sure you’re not double booked.”

   * What's the goal? 
   * Where do you want to be 6 months from now with that?
   * What is your main priority right now [topic] wise?




Step 4: Problems


This question is very simple, no need to overcomplicate it. 


   * “Gotcha. What do you think you need the most right now to get [goal]?
   * “Understood. What seems to be the biggest challenge in getting that done for you right now?”


This is a great question because if you can provide what they need (if it’s literally your service), then you know they’re a good fit.


Step 5: Help


You know what they need so the next step is simple:


   * “Would you like some help?”
   * “Would you be opposed to [seeing how we can get you your dream outcome] this week?”




Step 6: Sell the Call


Pick a date and time or send them your calendly:
   * Awesome. Are you free right now? I got some space to hop on… 
   * “Awesome. Are you free [day/time] or would it be easier if I send my calendar over?”
   * Amazing. Grab a spot on my calendar here. [LINK] I just opened up a few extra spots for you.


Give them a reason to book right there and then.


   * “Cool, I’ll keep my window open while you choose a time. Mind telling me when you’ve found one that works?”
   * “Cool, let me know what email you book with so I can make sure you’re not double booked.”
