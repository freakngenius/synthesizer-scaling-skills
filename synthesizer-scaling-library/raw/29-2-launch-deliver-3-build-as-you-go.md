The Program Hierarchy 
1. Phases - key milestones someone has to hit in order to reach their dream outcome
2. Modules - the knowledge needed in order to take action and move through each phase
3. Actions - the steps you take with the knowledge you have gained




Just in Time Delivery:
* We build onboarding right away
* Once people buy, we build Week 1
* As they’re going through Week 1, we build Week 2
* As they’re going through Week 2, we build Week 3
* Etc.



How we build Synthesizer Scaling Program:
1. We build the outline of the phases
2. We created an onboarding welcome video to get people ready
3. We synthesized the phases into a rough outline of the modules we think will be needed
4. Each week, we improved the modules and created the training for them
5. We presented that week's phase live on a call
6. We delivered the training for the week through pre recorded training videos
7. We gave specific action items for people to do

1. Build program outline

3. Build as you go by chunking out modules
* Then we took each of those and made a google doc for them 
* There we put the minimum viable training needed for you to accomplish it and move on 
* Finally we filmed a walkthrough and made it live




Innovate on your Product

* We have given you the proven roadmap. It's your turn to innovate on your area of expertise and how you help people.
