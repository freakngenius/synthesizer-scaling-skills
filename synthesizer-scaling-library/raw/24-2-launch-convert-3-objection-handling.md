## Doc: Objection Handling

The purpose of this training: 
* Most people think objection handling starts when the price is presented. They are wrong. 
* Great sales people discover the objections and handle them early on before the price is presented.
* Then after price is presented, the objections that are left are easier to handle


Our Sales Call Process: 
      0. Pre Call Prep
1. Intro and framing
2. Discovery 
3. Transition 
4. Pitch
5. Commitment
6. Investment
7. Objections + Close
________________
Sales Process 5-7:

        5. Commitment
* 2-5 min
* Create dialogue and diagnose where the prospect currently sits on your process
* Increase the prospects confidence in you and make them itch to just know how much it costs so they can get started
* Eliminate objections around: “Will this work for me”, “I want something else”, “This isn’t the right time”.
6. Investment
* 1 min
* Present the investment in a simple clear way with an authentic price anchor


7. Objections and Close
* 5-15 min
* Collect PIF (Paid in full) as first priority 
* Downsell to payment plan only if needed
* Handle all objections
* When we can’t close, we still close. BAMFAM (book a meeting from a meeting)
________________


Overview of objection handling and principles


What is an objection? 
   * Objections are doubts and concerns manifesting as an excuse to not take action.
   * The objection reframe: Objections are opportunities to help.




How most of you handle objections: 
Lead: “Oh I got to sleep on it… can we chat tmr?”
You: “yea cool, send me a DM tomorrow then?” 


Lead: “Do you do this through group coaching?”
You: “Yes! We host a couple group coaching calls every week “
Lead: “Oh I hate group coaching… last time I joined group coaching it sucked. I said I would never buy group coaching again.”
You: “... nuggets.”




How you should handle objections: 
1. Acknowledge objection to give comfort
2. Add to their objection and associate with buying behaviour
3. Ask a clarifying question to regain control and bring them back 


        Example: 
        
        Lead: “Oh I got to sleep on it… can we chat tmr?” 
You: “100% my man. I definitely want you to think about this. I’ve actually noticed all our best clients are the ones who don’t rush into a decision. Now, around the process, what precisely do you need to think about that I can provide you more clarity on?
Lead: “Honestly, it's just a little pricey for me that's all.” 
You: BOOM real objection discovered. Handle that.




Key tips:
* Double tap: People never lie to your face twice. 
* Whenever you get an objection, IMMEDIATELY RESPOND: “Okay, what else?” 
* Isolate the objection: “Okay cool, so if [objection] was completely out of the picture, then you’re saying you would be ready to rock and roll today?” 
* REASK for the sale every time. The more times you ask, the more shots you take, the higher the likelihood one goes in.
