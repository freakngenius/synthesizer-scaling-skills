Sales Process 4: The Bridge Framework
* 2-5 min pitch
* In the previous training we learned how to create a Gap during the discovery of the sales call
* The pitch is the “Bridge” that you are selling them to get across the Gap to their desired situation
   * Create a LOGICAL bridge from current situation to desired situation and away from pain.
   * CLARITY not CONFUSION. Confusion kills.
   * You want them to have a lightbulb moment. When you do this right you will see them bobbing their head up and down like “wow that makes sense”.
  





The 3 Sections of Your Bridge:
1. Promise of Transformation 
2. The 3 Bridge Pillars
3. Delivery across the bridge




1. Promise of Transformation
* 1 sentence that clearly articulates the transformation you provide.
* You describe the dream state on the other side of the gap the prospect wants to reach
* Key:
   * Makes it result based not feature based
   * Gives conviction, certainty and clarity
   * Makes it boolean (tangible and proven to be either true or false)
* Synthesizers Promise of Transformation:
   * This is going to be a 3 phase program that walks you through exactly how to scale your business to $10,000 to $100,000/month in about 6 months.
   * This is going to be a 3 phase program that walks you through exactly how to go from feast famine cycles and struggling with client attraction to consistent reliable $10-100k months in about 6 months.


2. The 3 Bridge Pillars
* 3 pillars that progress from one to the next in a unique way that logically explains how you get your client across the Gap
* Key:
   * Short and sweet
   * Proof for every pillar
   * Gives conviction, certainty, and clarity
   * New mechanism: Not an improvement offer
   * Logical: Each pillar leads to the next in a way that just makes sense
   * Elements confusion. Creates clarity
* Synthesizer Scaling Pillars: 
   * Business Backbone
   * Rapid Launch Protocol
   * Scalable Systems
      * Scalable Attraction Systems
      * Scalable Conversion Systems
      * Scalable Delivery Systems
* Steps to presenting your pillar:
   * State the pillar: “
      * The first thing we do is our Business Backbone.” 
   * Explain the pillar: 
      * “Now earlier in the call you mentioned not feeling confident or clear in your offer. And after working with hundreds of educational creators, we’ve noticed that their business is like a ship. It can’t set sail and leave harbour without confidence and a clear destination. That's why we first get crystal clear on the backbone and fundamentals of your business. So when we start applying leverage to it later, we know it will scale quickly and efficiently. 
   * Ask a commitment question: 
      * “Now, what stands out to you about that?”


3. Delivery Across the Bridge
* Simple explanation of how the pillars are delivered and what it tangibly looks like
* From “what” to “how”: Don’t let them ask, address it early and with certainty 
* Key:
   * Benefits not features 
   * Remove negatively connotated phrasings and wordings. 
      * Eg: Course to Program. Group calls to Backbone Review Sessions. Homework to Implementation Worksheets. Community support to Asynchronous 1:1 support.
* Use to eliminate common objections:
   * “How fast am I going to get support” 
   * What happens after? 
   * Will I get lost / am I going to have enough support to be able to apply general —> specific? Etc etc. 
* Synthesizers Delivery:
   * Now, in terms of this will work, first you get lifetime access to our Scaling Roadmap program that will walk you through exactly what you need to scale to $10,000-$100,000/month. Then you also get 1:1 access to me at any time through our private community where you can get asynchronous support so you don’t have to wait to book a call and wait to ask questions. Finally we host multiple weekly implementation sessions where you can get even more support and hand holding to ensure you have everything you need to hit your goals.

How to Craft Your Pitch


* Open your Business Backbone
* Your “outcome” becomes your “1. Promise of Transformation”
* Your “unique mechanism” becomes “2. Pitch Pillars”
   * Pillar 1: What is the first quick win I need to do with clients to get them to [outcome]? 
   * Pillar 2: What is the core problem they are facing and how do I solve it in a new unique way?
   * Pillar 3: After they have these 2 problems solved, what is the next thing they run into/want and how do I help them get it?
* Your “deliverables” become “3. Delivery Across the Bridge”
* Copy the template below
* Get help from us to fine tune it

The Pitch Template: 


Promise of Transformation:
* Purpose of 
   * To make the pitch result focused (vacation, not plane ticket) and tangible and provide clarity.
* Template 1: 
   * “This is a 3-phase program to help you get from [current situation] to [desired situation] in about [timeframe].
Pillar 1 (Old vs New Mechanism)
* Purpose of Pillar 1:
   *  Explain why what they have tried hasn’t worked and why what you do will get them what they want. This is a foundational step they need to take in order to solve their core problem.
* Template: 
   * “The first pillar is called [name]. You see, after [proof] we’ve noticed that most people are doing [old way]... which leads to [problems], which results in [painful thing].  What we do instead is [pillar description], so you can [benefit] and [benefit]”. What stands out to you about that?
* Template 2: 
   * “Now earlier on the call you mentioned [problem/pain]. Well after [proof] we’ve noticed that where most people are doing wrong is [old way]. So what we do instead is [pillar description, so you can [benefit] and [benefit].  What stands out to you about that?”
Pillar 2 (Logical step into Core Problem + next mechanism)
* Purpose of Pillar 2:
   * Solve the core issue or biggest problem they are facing in a way that logically transitions from the first step.
* Template: 
   * Now that we have taken care of [pillar 1] you’ve already got [benefit] and benefit] and are ready for our second phase, [pillar 2 name]. Here we’ve noticed after [proof] that most people need [description of pillar 2]. So that's why we do [solution] so you can get [result]. What questions do you have about that? 
Pillar 3 (Final logical step and future pace)
* Purpose of pillar 3: 
   * Answer the question of “what happens after I achieve success?”. Solve the next logical problem after they solve their main core problem.
* Template: 
   * “Now at this point in time you’re finally ready for [pillar 3]. Here, where we noticed people go wrong is once they have [pillar 1] and [pillar 2] setup they stop… This means they reach a ceiling on their growth they can’t get past. So what we do instead is [final mechanism]. This is actually what allowed one of my clients to [stop doing painful thing] and start [doing things they want] which ultimately resulted in [benefit of benefit]. Does that make sense?”
Delivery Across the Bridge
* Purpose of Delivery: 
   * Answer the “how does this all work?” question before the prospect can ask it. (without going too in depth and driving them out of the benefit brain and into the feature side of the brain).
* Template: 
   * “Now how this all works is first we will get you set up with [first deliverable.] This lets you get setup and get results right out of the gate. Now you also get [second deliverable] which means you will never get lost and will always have the 1:1 support you need. On top of all that you also get [third deliverable]. This means after everything starts working you have exactly what you need to continue taking this to the next level.”

Common Pitch Problems and Solutions:


* The biggest and main problem: You don’t actually know what you are doing and how to get people's results, therefore your understanding of your program isn’t good and your pitch is therefore bad.
   * Reps baby res.
* Long and confusing pitching. If you confuse you lose
   * Simple, short, sweet, easy to understand 
* Fancy hard to understand names. A unique mechanism isn’t unique because of a fancy name.
   * Clear unique name without being over the top. 
* Overreliance on pitch decks, slides, and fancy visuals. 
   * You need to be able to pitch at any point in time, with or without sharing your screen and reading from slides.
* Focusing on the features and the process, not the benefits and the result
* Treating it like a presentation where the lead isn’t involved
   * Dialogue and conversation not presentation. 
   * “What stands out to you about that?” “Do you think that's something that could help you?” “What questions do you have about that pillar?”
* Soft Unboolean pillars: Eg - We create content for in our launch protocol
   * Strong Boolean pillars: The third pillar is our rapid launch protocol. This is a challenge where the average person who goes through it gets 1-5 clients in 14 days.
* Avoiding or not including proof in the pitch
   * Every pillar has proof attached
* Delivering the exact same word for word pitch to every lead
   * Customizing the pitch based on what the prospect has said they are struggling with and what they want
      * “Earlier you mentioned struggling with getting enough calls booked. Well after working with hundreds of creators we’ve noticed that tends to be one of the core things holding them back from scaling their businesses past $10k/month. That's why we work on scalable attraction systems that reliably drive hundreds of qualified leads to your calendar on demand.”
