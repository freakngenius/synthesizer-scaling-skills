## Doc: Diagnosing and Solving Constraints

Diagnosing and Solving Constraints 


At this stage of the roadmap you have a lot on your plate.


One of the greatest ROI things you can do is fine tuning your time and your work so that you’re spending it on the highest leverage activities for your unique situation. 


This training will walk you through how to diagnose your number 1 constraint so that you can focus your attention on solving it and 10x your business.


________________






Step 1: Diagnosing Constraint


The first mistake 99% of us make is mis-diagnosing our biggest constraint. So many people waste time working on things that do not solve their biggest constraint and therefore don’t grow their business.


Ask: What is your biggest bottleneck? 
* Is it attracting enough eyeballs and booking calls?
* Is it closing on those calls and converting eyeballs? 
* Is it delivering to customers and scaling your delivery so you can do more of the first two? 


How to figure this out: (General rules)
* If you’re booking <2 calls a day your bottleneck is attract.
* If you’re closing at sub 20% your bottleneck is convert. Or if you have lots of eyeballs on content but struggling to get them to book a call your bottleneck is also convert.
* If you have 10+ clients (could be as low as 5) and it is taking up over 50% of your time, the bottleneck is delivery.
